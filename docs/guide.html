Chapter 01

Interface Architecture & Workflow Overview

Wildcat Studio separates the workspace into four coordinated zones so critical tolerances remain legible while iterating on wildcat geometry:

  • Top Action Bar: Preset browser (Cmd/Ctrl+O), wildcatting wizard (Cmd/Ctrl+W), shop print sheets (Cmd/Ctrl+P), display calibration, comparison overlay, and CAD exports.
  • Central 2D Blueprint & 3D Lathe Canvas: Smooth pan/zoom SVG blueprint drafting plus Three.js solid and half-cutaway 3D rendering.
  • Parametric Drawer (Cmd/Ctrl+B): Structured datum inputs for rim/headspace, case body, shoulder, neck, primer pocket, freebore, leade, and parabolic web thickness.
  • Volumetric & Ballistic Telemetry HUD: Real-time overflow water capacity, bullet shank displacement, net powder volume, bore index, expansion ratio, and Miller twist stability.
Chapter 02

Encyclopedic Cartridge Database (Cmd/Ctrl+O)

Wildcat Studio bundles 396 deduplicated SAAMI, CIP, and historic wildcat presets organized across canonical categories (Military Service, Precision Match/PRS, AR-15 Tactical, Hunting, Magnums & ELR, Dangerous Game, Straight-Wall, Handgun/PCC, Classic Wildcats & Ackley Improved, and Rimfire).

Custom designs save persistently to local storage (Cmd/Ctrl+S) or export cleanly as portable .wildcat JSON files.

Chapter 03

2D CAD Blueprint Drafting & In-Place Popovers

Every blueprint dimension callout can be edited inline by clicking the dimension label on the canvas:

  • Precision Steppers: Nudge dimensions cleanly in inches or millimeters without leaving the drawing view.
  • Geometric Validation: Automatically preserves valid axial section continuity and radial case taper boundaries.
  • Undo/Redo History: Revert edits with Cmd/Ctrl+Z or redo via Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z / Ctrl+Y.
Chapter 04

Volumetric Physics & 1,000-Slice Simpson Integration

Both the Rust backend and TypeScript geometry engine share aligned 1,000-slice Simpson 1/3 numerical integration to eliminate approximation drift between UI previews and saved CAD exports:

V = (h / 3) * SUM [ A(x_2k) + 4*A(x_2k+1) + A(x_2k+2) ]
where h = L_cavity / 1000 and internal area A(x) subtracts brass web thickness, rimfire/centerfire primer pocket reliefs, extractor grooves (omitted cleanly on rimfire cases), and parabolic wall thickness taper.

Reported outputs include Gross Overflow Water Capacity (grains H2O and cm3), Seated Shank Displacement, Net Usable Powder Space, and CIP axial delta metrics.

Chapter 05

Projectile Library & Seating Depth Solver

Choose from bundled Sierra, Hornady, Berger, and Lapua projectiles or define custom bullet dimensions (diameter, length, boat-tail length, bearing surface, meplat, weight, and G1/G7 BC).

Adjusting Cartridge Overall Length (COAL) live updates base-to-ogive engagement, seating depth past the neck-shoulder junction ("donut zone" checks), and Miller gyroscopic stability (Sg).

Chapter 06

Wildcatting & Case Forming Engine (Cmd/Ctrl+W)

  • Caliber Matrix Necking: Neck parent brass up or down while estimating resulting neck wall thickness and outside neck-turning requirements.
  • Ackley Improved 40-Degree Blowout: Sharpens shoulder geometry to 40 degrees and minimizes body taper while preserving fireforming headspace datum alignment.
  • Action-Length Truncation: Shortens long-action brass cleanly for short-action or AR magazine constraints.
Chapter 07

Chamber Reamers & Barrel Setback Solver

Generate toolmaker-ready reamer drawings and requisition blocks for PTG, Dave Manson, JGS Precision, and Clymer, complete with freebore length, throat angle, pilot bushing sizing, and chamber clearances.

The integrated setback solver evaluates axial barrel setback distances against existing chamber contours to determine exact thread-shoulder cleanup margins.

Chapter 08

1:1 True-Scale Physical Display Calibration

Calibrate monitor PPI once against an ISO ID-1 credit card (3.370" x 2.125"), 1.000" dial caliper span, 50.00 mm metric rule, or US quarter (0.955") so 1:1 mode matches physical brass held against the display.

Chapter 09

Dual Tolerance Envelope Mode (MMC vs LMC)

Overlay Maximum Material Condition cartridge envelopes inside chamber contours with live clearance deltas (Δ) across base diameter, shoulder junction, neck clearance, rim thickness, and overall chamber headspace.
